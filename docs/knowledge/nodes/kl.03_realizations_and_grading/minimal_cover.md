# Minimal graded free covers

A minimal free cover of a finite nonnegatively graded module \(M\) is a finite nonnegatively graded free module \(F\) together with a graded surjection \(\pi:F\to M\) such that
\[
\ker\pi\subseteq A_{>0}F.
\]
Here \(A_{>0}\) is the ideal generated by positive-degree homogeneous elements. Thus the cover has no redundant generators after reduction modulo the positive-degree ideal.
