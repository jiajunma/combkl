# 4. Localization at a root plane

The goal is an actual local sheaf decomposition, including the endpoint restriction maps. A comparison of stalk ranks alone is not enough.

## Local models and the surviving graph

Read [[node:kl.notes.dihedral_kl]] for the dihedral Hecke calculation, then [[node:kl.notes.dihedral_models]] for the rank-one model and the product formula for its upward kernels. [[node:kl.notes.sheaf_localization]] explains why edge modules outside the chosen plane vanish, and why kernels and images can be computed before or after localization.

After reducing by a fixed edge label, the remaining local ring is a discrete valuation ring. Products of surviving root labels are therefore measured by the counts from Chapter 3.

## Extending sections and splitting the sheaf

[[node:kl.sheaves.relative_flabbiness]] gives the extension statement for regions closed under surviving edges. This supplies the local flabbiness input even when the relevant relative upper set is not ambiently upper.

Finally, [[node:kl.local.localized_decomposition]] constructs the sum of dihedral models and a sheaf isomorphism to it. The same edge component of that isomorphism will be used for both endpoint images in the next chapter.
