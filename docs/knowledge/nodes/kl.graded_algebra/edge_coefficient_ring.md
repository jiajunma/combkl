# Edge coefficient rings in symmetric and polynomial coordinates

For a scalar label \(r\) in a commutative coefficient ring \(R\), the quotient \(R/(r)\) consists of scalar classes modulo multiples of \(r\). It is the coefficient ring acting on an edge module annihilated by that label.

Let \(k\) be a field, \(V\) a \(k\)-vector space and \(\iota:V\to\operatorname{Sym}_k(V)\) its symmetric-algebra generator map. For \(a\in V\), the **edge coefficient ring** is
\[
C_a=\operatorname{Sym}_k(V)/(\iota(a)).
\]
The quotient map sends a polynomial \(f\) to its class \([f]\). In particular, reducing another linear label means taking \([\iota(v)]\).

The ideal \((\iota(a))\) is generated by the one scalar \(\iota(a)\). The associated vector subspace is the root line \(ka\). The [[node:kl.algebra.root_quotient_coordinates|edge-quotient coordinate theorem]] identifies \(C_a\) with \(\operatorname{Sym}_k(V/ka)\).

## The actual symmetric presentation

For the finite-generator Coxeter development, let \(V_0\) be the original root space, \(V\) the extended real realization, and \(i_0:V_0\hookrightarrow V\) its embedding. Put \(A_{\mathrm{sym}}=\operatorname{Sym}_{\mathbb R}(V)\).

For a positive root \(a\in V_0\), the source's EdgeCoefficient is
\[
\overline A_a=A_{\mathrm{sym}}/(\iota(i_0a)).
\]
Its distinguished killed scalar is the embedded positive-root label.

## The polynomial presentation

A chosen finite real basis of \(V\) supplies
\[
\Phi:A_{\mathrm{sym}}\xrightarrow{\sim}A,
\qquad A=\mathbb R[X_1,\ldots,X_N].
\]
Write
\[
\lambda(v)=\Phi(\iota(i_0v)),\qquad r_a=\lambda(a).
\]
The source calls \(r_a\) polynomialRoot. Its polynomial edge coefficient ring, named EdgeRing, is
\[
E_a=A/(r_a).
\]

The coordinate isomorphism carries the principal ideal
\((\iota(i_0a))\) onto \((r_a)\). Thus it induces an isomorphism
\[
\psi_a:E_a\xrightarrow{\sim}\overline A_a,\qquad
[f]\longmapsto[\Phi^{-1}(f)].
\]
The source's edgeRingEquiv has this direction. For another positive root \(c\),
\[
\psi_a([\lambda(c)])=[\iota(i_0c)].
\]
The [[node:kl.algebra.root_quotient_coordinates|edge-quotient coordinate theorem]] proves the coordinate formula, the domain and unique-factorization properties, and primality of reduced labels of distinct positive roots.

## Scalars on an edge module

For an actual boundary-sheaf edge with root label \(r_a\), that scalar annihilates the edge module. The coefficient action therefore factors through \(E_a\):
\[
[f]\cdot m=fm.
\]
Changing \(f\) by a multiple of \(r_a\) leaves this product unchanged. The [[node:kl.sheaves.edge_coefficient_module|actual edge-module theorem]] constructs this action and proves freeness over \(E_a\) using the upper stalk's basis.

For \(A=k[X,Y]\) and \(r_a=X\), the ring is \(k[Y]\), and the label \(X+2Y\) reduces to \(2Y\). These are the scalar classes acting on the corresponding quotient edge module.
