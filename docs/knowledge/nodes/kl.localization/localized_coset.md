# Localized labels and the coset sheaf

Fix a finite-generator Coxeter system, a root plane \(P\) in its original root space \(V_0\), and a plane basis for \(P\). Choose a finite real basis of the extended realization, giving the polynomial coefficient algebra \(A\).

Let \(\lambda:V_0\to A\) send a root-space vector to its degree-one polynomial label. Let \(\mathfrak p_P\) be the prime ideal generated by the labels of vectors in \(P\), and put
\[
R_P=A_{\mathfrak p_P},\qquad \eta:A\to R_P.
\]
The **localized linear-label map** is
\[
\lambda_P:V_0\to R_P,\qquad \lambda_P(v)=\eta(\lambda(v)).
\]
This is localizedLinear, a real-linear map.

Choose a least-length representative \(a_0\) in \(D_Pa_0\) and a top \(b\). Let \(K\) be the finite relative coset graph and
\(j:K\to\operatorname{BruhatGraph}([1,b])\) its graph map.

## The coset labels

For a relative edge \(a\), let \(\beta_{j_E(a)}\) be the ambient positive root of its ambient edge. Define
\[
\alpha^K_a=\eta(\lambda(\beta_{j_E(a)}))
=\lambda_P(\beta_{j_E(a)}).
\]
This is cosetLabel. The equality between the induced and ambient edge roots identifies this also with the localized label of the induced edge root; the [[node:kl.local.localized_coset_properties|localized coset properties theorem]] proves that correspondence.

## Scalar extension followed by pullback

For any \(A\)-linear sheaf \(C\) on \([1,b]\), its **localized coset sheaf** is
\[
\mathcal C_{P,a_0,b}=j^*(C_{R_P}),
\qquad C_{R_P}=R_P\otimes_A C.
\]
In coordinates,
\[
\mathcal C^d=R_P\otimes_A C^{da_0},
\qquad
\mathcal C^a=R_P\otimes_A C^{j_E(a)}.
\]
Both restrictions are the tensor-extended ambient restrictions, with endpoint modules identified by the graph map.

The construction is over the local ring \(R_P\) as an ungraded sheaf. Its two operations have separate roles: scalar extension changes the coefficient ring, while pullback selects the finite relative graph and its actual restrictions.

## A coefficient-ring illustration

Take \(A=\mathbb R[\alpha,\beta,\gamma]\) and \(\mathfrak p_P=(\alpha,\beta)\). The element \(\gamma\) is invertible in \(R_P\). An upper quotient labelled by \(\gamma\) becomes
\[
R_P/(\gamma)=0.
\]
A label in the plane ideal belongs to the maximal ideal of \(R_P\). The [[node:kl.local.localized_coset_properties|localized coset properties theorem]] uses this label distinction to verify the surviving-edge pullback hypotheses.

## Relation to the OpenAI notes

**Relative extension through an explicit graph map.**

**OpenAI notes:** The coset intersection is a finite relative lower ideal. A relative upper subset is closed under the surviving plane-labelled edges. Descending ambient extension gives relative flabbiness after localization. [loc:relative-flabbiness](https://github.com/jiajunma/combkl/blob/108243e05ed46235bf82dc30d4d90c794579776f/sources/openai-math/preprints/Combinatorial-Invariance-of-Kazhdan-Lusztig-Polynomials-September-24-2026/build/04-localization.tex#L226).

**Lean (primary):** The same descending extension is factored into extend_closedUnder and a graph-pullback theorem. The coset map supplies vertex injectivity and explicit outgoing lifts; the root/unit criterion makes all outside edge modules zero. A separate theorem records selected-kernel equality under that pullback. [Sheaves:106](https://github.com/jiajunma/combkl/blob/108243e05ed46235bf82dc30d4d90c794579776f/sources/openai-math/lean/OAI/RepresentationTheory/KazhdanLusztig/Sheaves.lean#L106), [MinimalExtensions:1520](https://github.com/jiajunma/combkl/blob/108243e05ed46235bf82dc30d4d90c794579776f/sources/openai-math/lean/OAI/RepresentationTheory/KazhdanLusztig/MinimalExtensions.lean#L1520), [PlaneLocalization:497](https://github.com/jiajunma/combkl/blob/108243e05ed46235bf82dc30d4d90c794579776f/sources/openai-math/lean/OAI/RepresentationTheory/KazhdanLusztig/PlaneLocalization.lean#L497), [PlaneLocalization:591](https://github.com/jiajunma/combkl/blob/108243e05ed46235bf82dc30d4d90c794579776f/sources/openai-math/lean/OAI/RepresentationTheory/KazhdanLusztig/PlaneLocalization.lean#L591), [TerminalKernels:393](https://github.com/jiajunma/combkl/blob/108243e05ed46235bf82dc30d4d90c794579776f/sources/openai-math/lean/OAI/RepresentationTheory/KazhdanLusztig/TerminalKernels.lean#L393).

**Role in the proof:** Relative order determines the upper subsets on the coset, while the ambient graph supplies the extension. The localized stalks and edge maps remain the actual tensor-extended ambient modules, so selected kernels and their edge images retain their embeddings.
