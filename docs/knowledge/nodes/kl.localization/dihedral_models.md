# Rank-one structure sheaves on dihedral intervals

Let \(D\) be the induced dihedral Coxeter system of a root plane in a finite-generator ambient Coxeter system \(W\). Its subgroup inclusion \(\phi:D\hookrightarrow W\) sends reflections to ambient reflections. Choose a real basis of the ambient extended realization and let \(A=\mathbb R[X_\sigma]\) be the polynomial coefficient ring on that basis.

On a relative edge \(x\to y\), the reflection is \(yx^{-1}\) in \(D\). Its induced label \(\alpha_e\) is the linear polynomial of the embedded positive ambient root representing \(\phi(yx^{-1})\). Thus the coefficients and labels come from the actual ambient realization.

Fix an interval \([1,b]\) in \(D\) and a center \(c\le_D b\). Its structure model \(\mathcal O_c\) has stalk \(A\) at each \(x\le_D c\) and zero outside that lower ideal. An edge supported below \(c\) has module \(A/(\alpha_e)\), with the endpoint maps given by reduction modulo its label.

A section is therefore a collection of polynomials satisfying the edge congruences
\[
f_x\equiv f_y\pmod{\alpha_e}.
\]
The model is generated by sections and is flabby for relative upper sets. Its edge quotient rule is literal. The [[node:kl.detail.dihedral_model_properties|interpolation theorem]] proves these properties for the induced labels. The notes relate the rank-one description to [[node:kl.notes.dihedral_kl|the dihedral polynomial identity]] \(P^D_{x,c}=1\).

For a supported vertex and a set \(F\) of its outgoing supported edges, the kernel conditions say that its polynomial is divisible by every \(\alpha_e\), \(e\in F\). Distinct incident reflections have nonproportional linear labels. Since the coefficient ring is a unique factorization domain,
\[
L_x(F)=\left(\prod_{e\in F}\alpha_e\right)A.
\]
Pairwise nonassociate prime linear factors make simultaneous divisibility equivalent to divisibility by their product.

After root-plane localization and reduction by one edge label, the remaining plane labels are uniformizers times units. The valuation of this product becomes the number of remaining supported edges. This is the precise passage from dihedral combinatorics to the local comparison of sheaf images.

The induced-label interpolation and quotient properties supply the model data for local decomposition. The polynomial calculation supplies the correspondence with the character-based description in the notes.
