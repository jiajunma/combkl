The main argument proves two types of statements together at the R-polynomial stage: invariance of the normalized R-data and freeness of the selected kernels that occur in schedule suffixes. Smaller intervals supply the upper-kernel freeness needed for comparison at the current rank.

The forward comparison gives inequalities between the relevant polynomial expressions. The argument in the opposite direction and the smaller-interval identities isolate the remaining R-entry and force equality. Once the R-data agree, character reciprocity makes the comparison endpoints exact. Numerical equality then becomes image equality, and reverse traversal recovers the suffix-kernel freeness at the same rank.

A separate induction then treats the canonical KL family. The common R-data give the same reciprocity equation; the strict degree bound eliminates a remaining reciprocal difference. [[node:kl.main.finite_model_transport|Finite-support reduction]] transfers the finite-generator conclusion to arbitrary Coxeter systems. The reading route keeps the simultaneous R/freeness induction and the later canonical-polynomial induction distinct.
