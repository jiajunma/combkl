The global comparison takes place in one edge module over the quotient by the current root label. Assuming the selected upper kernel is free over the polynomial algebra, saturation makes its image free over the edge quotient ring. The injective correction action makes the corrected upper image a free target in the same module.

The outgoing label product sends every upper-stalk vector into the selected kernel, and surjectivity of the upper restriction sends its multiples of every edge vector into the upper image. Multiplying by the correction lift gives one global multiplier with nonzero reduction. Every prime divisor of this reduction is associated to a reduced root.

The [[node:kl.local.ambient_plane_scaled_images|ambient local comparison]] supplies a multiplier outside the corresponding plane prime for each lower-image vector. Quotient divisibility translates that into a witness not divisible by the chosen prime. Prime-factor removal in the free target then proves global membership. The exact schedule-suffix identities apply this inclusion to the two remaining endpoint condition sets.
