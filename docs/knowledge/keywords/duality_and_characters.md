A homogeneous free stalk has a character recording the degrees of its basis vectors. The proof also studies the full outgoing kernel at that vertex. Section duality relates these kernels to dual stalk data, with the grading shift supplied by the actual pairing.

The word construction first provides controlled characters and pairings. Decomposition into boundary-sheaf pieces then gives the character relations for the actual boundary sheaf. The reciprocity theorem expresses the full-kernel and stalk characters through the normalized R-family. Scaling these characters gives the initial and final values of a complete edge schedule.

These are characters of the constructed sheaf modules. Their endpoint identities are used when the R-comparison becomes exact in the [[node:kl.comparison.rtilde_invariance|simultaneous invariance induction]]. Canonical KL identification is a later polynomial step. The reading sequence keeps the character definition, the duality argument and the endpoint substitution connected in their proof order.
