---
title: The Tits form and the extended realization
primary_topic: kl.realizations
---

For the finite-generator part of the proof, the Tits form is a bilinear form determined by the Coxeter matrix on the vector space with one simple-root basis vector for each generator. Its simple reflections act by subtracting the appropriate normal-functional multiple of the simple root. Real roots are the images of simple roots under this action.

The form can have a radical. The extended realization adds the dual of that radical and extends the form to a nondegenerate one. This makes the normal functionals of independent roots independent and supports the linear algebra that recovers a root plane from a product of two distinct reflections.

The resulting geometry has direct uses in the main proof. Positive roots label reflection edges, root planes define the dihedral subgroups used in local comparison, and the symmetric algebra of the extended space provides the polynomial coefficient ring. The extension is therefore part of both the geometric and the module-theoretic constructions.
