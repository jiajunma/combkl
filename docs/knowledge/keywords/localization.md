title: Localization from root labels to actual edge images
primary_topic: kl.localization

A root plane determines a linear prime ideal in the polynomial coefficient algebra. Localizing at this ideal makes every root label outside the plane a unit. The corresponding edge modules then vanish under the upper-quotient rule, while the plane labels remain available for the relative coset calculation.

Flat scalar extension preserves the finite section and selected-kernel constructions. Pullback to the surviving coset graph identifies the remaining equations and both endpoint maps. The [[node:kl.local.localized_decomposition|localized boundary decomposition]] supplies a finite sum of structure models on this graph, with an isomorphism preserving all restrictions.

Each supported model has selected kernels generated by products of labels. After reduction by the current edge label, the remaining plane labels are associated to one reference root. The [[node:kl.paths.later_count|transported count theorem]] compares their exponents, giving the [[node:kl.local.ambient_plane_scaled_images|ambient local comparison]]. This equality supplies a multiplier outside the plane prime in the [[node:kl.local.edge_inclusion|global edge inclusion]] argument.
